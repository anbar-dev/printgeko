About PrintGeko

A shorter way to choose a 3D printer.

PrintGeko is a small editorial site for people who want a practical 3D printer recommendation without comparing dozens of models and specifications.

How the recommendations work

We start with the job: beginner printing, miniatures, functional parts, multiple colors, a smaller budget or large-format projects. Each page gives one primary recommendation, the main trade-off and only the alternatives that are useful for that specific decision.

Research combines manufacturer specifications, current product information and recurring recommendations from the 3D-printing community. We do not claim hands-on testing of every product listed, and prices, stock and product versions can change.

What PrintGeko is not

It is not a manufacturer, retailer or repair service. Product links may take you to Amazon, where the final product details, price, availability and purchase terms are controlled by Amazon and the seller.

Editorial promise: explain the best fit quickly, show the limitation before the click and keep the shortlist useful.
