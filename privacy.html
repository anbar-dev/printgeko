Privacy

Privacy at a glance.

PrintGeko is a static editorial website. We do not provide accounts, comments, contact forms or on-site payments.

Analytics

PrintGeko uses Google Analytics to understand general traffic and improve the site. Depending on your browser, region and consent settings, Google may process information such as page views, device and browser details, approximate location and interactions with the site. Google processes this information under its own terms and policies.

Amazon links

Some links take you to Amazon.com. After you leave PrintGeko, Amazon and its service providers may process information according to Amazon’s own privacy notice and cookie settings. PrintGeko does not receive your Amazon account, payment or order details.

Hosting and technical data

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages and may generate ordinary technical logs needed to deliver web pages securely and reliably. PrintGeko does not sell personal information and does not intentionally collect sensitive personal data through this site.

Your choices

You can limit cookies and analytics through your browser settings and any consent controls shown by your browser or region. Blocking analytics will not prevent you from reading the site or visiting Amazon links.

Important: this page is a general privacy notice for a small static site, not legal advice. Before paid promotion or substantial traffic from regulated jurisdictions, review the site’s analytics consent and privacy requirements for the locations you target.
