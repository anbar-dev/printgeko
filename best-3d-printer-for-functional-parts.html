What’s the best 3D printer for functional parts?

For brackets, enclosures, prototypes and parts that need to survive real use, the important question is not only speed. Look at the frame, temperature control, enclosure and materials you can actually print reliably.

Short answer: choose the QIDI Q2.

It is the technical pick when you want a heated chamber, a high-temperature nozzle and more room for engineering materials. Choose the P1S Combo instead if simplicity matters more than maximum material range.
