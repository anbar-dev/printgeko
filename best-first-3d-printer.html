For beginners

What’s the easiest 3D printer to start with?

You don’t need the fastest or most complicated machine. You need one that gets calibrated, prints reliably and leaves you with fewer things to troubleshoot.

Short answer: choose the Bambu Lab A1 Mini.

It is the cleanest starting point for small PLA, PETG and TPU projects. Choose something else only if you already know you need a larger build area or an enclosed printer.
