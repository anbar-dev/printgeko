Before buying a replacement

Compatibility comes before the discount.

For hotends and nozzles

  • Match the exact printer model.
  • Check nozzle diameter and material.
  • Confirm connector, voltage and mounting style.
  • Do not treat a generic “0.4 mm nozzle” as universal.

For resin equipment

  • Match the printer’s build area and workflow.
  • Use gloves, eye protection and ventilation.
  • Never pour uncured resin or contaminated wash water down a drain.
  • Follow the resin safety data sheet and also local disposal rules.
